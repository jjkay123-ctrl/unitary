1. PRIVACY POLICY

Welcome to Unitary. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting personal information collected through our platform.

1.1 Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, Unitary may collect:

  • Name and display name.
  • Username and profile information.
  • Email address and authentication information.
  • Profile pictures and other uploaded content.
  • Messages and content you choose to share.
  • Voice and video call-related information.
  • Device, browser and technical information.
  • Security logs and account activity.
  • Information submitted through support requests.

1.2 How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and manage accounts.
  • Provide social networking services.
  • Enable messaging and communication.
  • Enable voice and video calls.
  • Display profiles and shared content.
  • Protect users against fraud and abuse.
  • Maintain and improve platform performance.
  • Respond to support and legal requests.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations.

1.3 Messages and Communications

Unitary provides communication features, including private messaging and voice and video communication.

Messages and communication data may be processed or stored by Unitary and its service providers as necessary to operate the service, subject to applicable privacy laws.

Encryption Notice: Unitary may use encryption and other security technologies to protect information during transmission and storage. However, Unitary does not represent that all communications currently use end-to-end encryption. Do not assume that a message is accessible only to its sender and intended recipient unless that specific feature has been independently verified and clearly identified.

1.4 Data Sharing

Unitary does not intend to sell users' personal information. Information may be shared when necessary with:

  • Hosting and database service providers.
  • Authentication and security providers.
  • Communication and infrastructure providers.
  • Technical support providers.
  • Authorized legal or regulatory authorities where required by law.

1.5 Cookies and Technical Information

Unitary and its service providers may use cookies, local storage, session technologies and similar tools to maintain sessions, remember settings, improve security and operate the platform.

1.6 Data Security

We aim to apply reasonable technical and organizational security measures, including access controls, secure authentication, encryption where implemented, and monitoring for suspicious activity.

However, no internet-based platform can guarantee absolute security. Users should protect their login credentials and avoid sharing sensitive information unnecessarily.

1.7 Data Retention

Personal information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide services, maintain security, resolve disputes, meet legal obligations and enforce platform policies.

Retention periods may differ depending on the type of information and applicable legal requirements.

1.8 Children's Privacy

Unitary will apply age restrictions and safeguards appropriate to the applicable laws in the countries where it operates.

Where parental consent is legally required, the platform will respect those requirements. Users must provide accurate age information and must not misrepresent their eligibility.

1.9 International Data Transfers

As Unitary expands internationally, personal information may be processed in countries other than the country where a user resides.

Where required, appropriate safeguards and legal transfer requirements will be considered and implemented.

1.10 User Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, users may have the right to:

  • Request access to their personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of personal information.
  • Object to certain processing activities.
  • Request restrictions on certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.
  • Submit complaints to an appropriate data protection authority.

1.11 Changes to This Policy

Unitary may update this Privacy Policy when services, technology or legal requirements change. Significant changes will be communicated through appropriate platform notices.

3. COMMUNITY GUIDELINES

Unitary aims to maintain a respectful, safe and inclusive environment for its worldwide community.

3.1 Respect Other Users

  • Treat other people with respect.
  • Do not engage in harassment or bullying.
  • Respect different opinions.
  • Do not threaten or intimidate others.

3.2 Prohibited Content

The platform prohibits content that violates applicable laws or creates serious safety risks, including:

  • Threats and targeted harassment.
  • Fraudulent schemes and scams.
  • Malicious software distribution.
  • Non-consensual sharing of private information.

  • Content promoting violence or unlawful activity.
  • Copyright-infringing material.

Users should report accounts, messages or content that violate these guidelines through available reporting features or the official support channel once published.

3.5 Appeals

Where an appeal process is available, affected users may request a review of certain enforcement decisions.

For operations involving Kenya, Unitary will assess and work towards compliance with applicable provisions of the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, its regulations and relevant guidance issued by the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC).

This includes considering:

  • Lawful processing of personal information.
  • Transparency and privacy notices.
  • Data subject rights.
  • Appropriate security safeguards.
  • Data retention and deletion requirements.
  • International data transfers.

5.7 Legal Cooperation

Unitary will cooperate with lawful regulatory and judicial processes as required by applicable law.
