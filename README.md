# 1. About Unitary

Unitary is a next-generation social networking and communication platform designed to connect people worldwide.

The platform aims to combine social networking, instant messaging, multimedia sharing, voice communication, video communication, communities, entertainment, artificial intelligence and digital services in one ecosystem.

Unitary aims to provide a secure, accessible, modern and user-friendly environment where people can communicate, collaborate, create and discover.

**Mission:** To bring people, ideas and opportunities together through technology.

**Vision:** To build a globally accessible digital community that makes communication and social interaction simpler.

## 4.1 Encryption

- End-to-end encrypted private messaging.
